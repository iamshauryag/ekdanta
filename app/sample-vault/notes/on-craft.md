title: On Craft
platform: note
---

Patience is not a mood. It is a way of refusing the first version of a sentence, again and again, until the one that is left could only have been written by you.

The draft that comes quickly is a map of what everyone already thinks. Revision is walking off the map.

Handwriting as a filter: the effort of the hand slows the sentence down enough for you to hear whether it is true. Typing removes the friction and lets the weak sentences through.
