---
title: Drafting Rules
platform: note
---

A rough draft is allowed to be clumsy for a day, as long as you come back to it with clean eyes. Never edit on the same day you draft; the person who wrote it is still defending it.

Cut the sentence you are proudest of and see whether the paragraph misses it. Usually it does not, and the pride was about the effort rather than the meaning.

When stuck, write the opposite claim as strongly as you can. Either it collapses, and you know your point is sound, or it does not, and you have found the essay you should be writing.
