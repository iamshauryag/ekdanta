---
title: Sunday Letters
platform: note
---

My grandmother wrote letters on Sunday afternoons at the same table by the window. She never recopied one. A crossed-out word, she said, is a small kind of honesty; it shows the reader where you changed your mind.

Reading her letters now I can watch her thinking happen on the page. The corrections are the most interesting part, because they are the only place where she argued with herself.
