title: The Unfinished Room

We moved into the flat in March and left one room unpainted for a year. It was meant to be temporary, a box room for whatever we had not decided about yet. By winter it had become the most honest room in the house, the only one that admitted we did not know what we were doing.

I think about that room whenever I finish a piece too neatly. I left the last paragraph undone for a week once, and it was the only honest one in the piece. The rest had been sanded until it said nothing that could be argued with.

Finishing is a decision, not a discovery. You do not find the end of an essay the way you find your keys; you choose to stop, and the choosing is part of what the essay says.
