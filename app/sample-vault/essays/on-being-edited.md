The first editor who really changed my writing never rewrote a sentence. She circled things and wrote a single word in the margin: why, or who, or really. It took me a year to understand that she was refusing to do my thinking for me.

A good edit is a question you cannot unhear. A bad edit is an answer you did not ask for, and it leaves you with a cleaner paragraph that belongs to someone else.

I keep a folder of the margins she left. Reading them now, I notice how rarely she told me what to say and how often she told me where I had stopped looking.
