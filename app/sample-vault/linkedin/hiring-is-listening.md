The best interview I ever ran had almost no questions in it.

I asked the candidate about a project they were proud of, and then I stopped talking. For twenty minutes they walked me through every wrong turn.

That was the interview. Not the answers. The wrong turns.

Most hiring processes are built to find people who sound right. The good ones are built to hear how someone thinks when they are not sure.
